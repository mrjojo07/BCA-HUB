Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellation and refund requests are handled for optional BCA SYNC Premium membership. Regular study notes and learning materials remain free.

1. Premium subscription

BCA SYNC Premium is offered at ₹49 for 6 months. The membership period begins after successful payment verification and activation.

2. Cancellation

Users may contact BCA SYNC to request cancellation of their Premium membership. Cancellation does not automatically guarantee a refund. Any recurring payment arrangement, if introduced, will be explained separately before a user agrees to it.

3. Refund eligibility

Refund requests may be reviewed in cases such as:

  • A duplicate payment for the same membership.
  • Payment was deducted but Premium access was not activated.
  • A confirmed technical payment error occurred.

4. Non-refundable cases

Once Premium access has been successfully activated and used, the payment is generally non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law or is approved after reviewing the circumstances.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds will normally be sent to the original payment method. The time taken for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider and the user's bank.

6. How to request help

For cancellation, refund, duplicate-payment or activation issues, email bca.sync.official@gmail.com. Include the payment date and transaction reference. Do not email your password, OTP, full card number or UPI PIN.

7. Policy changes

This policy may be updated if BCA SYNC's services or payment arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
