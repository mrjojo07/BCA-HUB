Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information BCA SYNC may handle when you use the website and how it is used.

1. Information you provide

If you create an account, the authentication service may process details such as your email address and account credentials. If you submit study material or feedback, the information and files you choose to provide may also be processed.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, save learning activity and resources, review student-submitted material, respond to feedback, maintain website functionality and understand general website usage.

3. Website analytics and device storage

BCA SYNC records limited interaction events such as page views and visits to semester, subject and resource sections. The website may store a randomly generated browser identifier in local storage and use session storage to avoid counting repeated page refreshes as separate page views within the same tab session. These technologies support basic site functionality and analytics.

4. Service providers

BCA SYNC uses Supabase services for account authentication, database functions and file storage. Information needed for these features may be processed by Supabase under its own service terms and privacy practices. The website may also load fonts from Google Fonts.

5. Sharing and publication

We do not offer personal information for sale. Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate the website, or disclosed where required by law. Material submitted for publication may become visible to website visitors after review. Do not include private information in files or feedback that you submit.

6. Cookies, advertising and third-party services

The website may use browser storage and similar technologies as described above. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are introduced in the future, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. This policy should be updated before advertising is enabled to explain the provider's current data practices and available choices.

7. Data security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect website information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet operational requirements and address legitimate issues, subject to applicable law and provider settings.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit optional feedback or files. For questions or requests about information associated with your BCA SYNC account, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

9. Children's privacy

BCA SYNC is an educational website for students. Users should avoid sharing unnecessary personal information, and younger users should seek help from a parent or guardian if they have questions about providing information online.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email bca.sync.official@gmail.com.
