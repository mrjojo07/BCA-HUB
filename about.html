ABOUT BCA SYNC

One hub. More possibilities.

BCA SYNC is a student-friendly learning space created to make Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) study resources easier to discover and use.

What you can find

Students can explore study notes, important questions, previous-year question papers and other learning resources organised to support their studies.

Our purpose

Our goal is to help BCA students learn concepts, practise skills and prepare for their academic work with greater confidence. We aim to keep the website simple, accessible and useful on mobile devices as well as computers.

Content and updates

Resources may be added or updated over time. Students who submit material should only share content they created or are permitted to share. Submitted material may be reviewed before it is published.

Questions or suggestions? Visit our Contact Us page.
